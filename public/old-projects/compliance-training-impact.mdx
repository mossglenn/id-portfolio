## Overview

This project addressed systemic issues in how compliance training was structured and delivered across institutions.

---

## The challenge

Problems included:

- outdated content
- poor alignment with workflows
- inconsistent implementation

---

## The solution

A redesigned curriculum emphasized:

- scenario-based decision making
- modular updates
- centralized governance

---

## Highlights

### Policy alignment
Training reflected current regulatory guidance.

### Usability improvements
Reduced friction for busy professionals.

### Scalable governance
Enabled rapid updates across institutions.
