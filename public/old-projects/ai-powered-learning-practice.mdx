## Overview

Staff in research programs must respond accurately and appropriately to sensitive questions, but traditional training provides limited opportunities for **practice with feedback**.

This project explored whether AI could support scalable, feedback-driven communication practice.

---

## The challenge

Key challenges included:

- evaluating free-text responses
- aligning feedback to approved language
- avoiding automated grading in sensitive contexts

---

## The solution

The prototype compares learner responses to **IRB-approved answers** using semantic similarity scoring, then:

- highlights gaps
- reinforces required concepts
- flags prohibited phrasing

---

## Highlights

### Practice at scale
Supports repeated practice without instructor involvement.

### Alignment with compliance requirements
Feedback is grounded in approved language frameworks.

### Transparent AI use
Learners see how their responses differ from targets.
